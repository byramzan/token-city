# Token City visual direction

## Target

Stylized semi-realistic low-poly game art: warm and inviting, with readable
silhouettes, believable materials, restrained painterly variation and no
photographic micro-noise. The normal gameplay camera, not an extreme close-up,
is the approval view.

Reference sheet: [`art-direction-reference.png`](art-direction-reference.png).
It is a non-runtime concept sheet; production materials are deterministic
CanvasTextures generated by `src/materialLibrary.js`.

## Palette and response

- Environment: grass `#91c966`, asphalt `#777a75`, warm pavers `#dfc99f`.
- Buildings: honey wood, muted terracotta brick, warm plaster and charcoal tech.
- Average saturation: medium-high outdoors, calmer indoors.
- Contrast: broad tonal patches; no razor-thin light/dark detail.
- Roughness: 0.68–0.98 for non-metal surfaces; metal 0.58 with 0.56 metalness.
- Bump: 0.006–0.028. It supports form and never replaces silhouette geometry.
- Wear: clean to lightly lived-in. No deep damage, grime layers or baked shadows.
- Edge treatment: low-poly silhouette, soft lighting response, no fake bevel noise.

## Detail scale

- Terrain/roads: 512 px tile, macro patches plus a small amount of broad detail.
- Building surfaces/floors: 256 px tile.
- Small furniture/props: 128–256 px shared material.
- Runtime signs: 512×256 or 512×160 only when text readability needs it.
- Maximum runtime surface source: 512×512. No 2K/4K/8K content.

## Lighting checks

Day, sunset, night and interior views must keep material families distinct.
Albedo contains no directional lighting. Night readability comes from the scene's
hemisphere/ambient fill and restrained emissive windows, not from baked highlights.

## Correct / incorrect examples

Correct: soft grass clusters, simplified wood grain, broad asphalt aggregate,
clean mortar scale, subtle fabric weave, single-layer glass.

Incorrect: photographic grass blades, tiny gravel speckle, deep parallax,
uniform plastic roughness, opaque black windows, repeated hard stains, coplanar
markings or surfaces, and any generated text/logo in a material tile.
