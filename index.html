My Hood

Whitepaper

A living low-poly town, built together by a community of holders. Your wallet is your key, your house is your mark on the map, and the whole city grows as more people join.

What is My Hood?

My Hood is a shared 3D town that lives in your browser. Everyone builds on the same map, so the streets, neighborhoods, and skyline you see are made by real players. Anyone can walk the city and look around for free — but to build and take part, you connect your wallet.

The city is not a way to earn money. It is a place to show that you are part of the community, to build something that is yours, and to watch a world grow around you.

Your wallet is your login

There are no passwords and no separate sign-ups. You connect your wallet, sign a free message to prove it is yours, and that becomes your account. Everything you build is tied to your wallet, so it is always waiting for you when you come back.

  • Connect a supported wallet to play.
  • Signing in is free and never asks for your secret phrase or private key.
  • No wallet, no building — but sightseeing is open to everyone.

Game Coins

Inside the city you spend Game Coins. You get them by depositing the project token, and you can turn Game Coins back into tokens whenever you like. Coins you earn from other players are yours to keep, spend, or withdraw.

  • Deposit tokens to receive Game Coins at a fixed, transparent rate — no deposit fee.
  • Withdraw Game Coins back to tokens at any time — no withdrawal fee.
  • Coins move between players when you buy and sell; the game never prints extra coins out of thin air.

Build your house

Your house is your personal landmark in the city. You choose its style, materials, and layout in the builder, then it takes its place on the shared map for everyone to see. You own one main house — make it count.

  • Design the look, then place it in the living town.
  • Every house sits on its own plot with a yard and a path to the street.
  • The city automatically draws roads and links similar houses into neighborhoods.

Grow floor by floor

Make your house bigger by adding floors. Each new floor adds living space and room for more family members — but building takes real time.

  • Each added floor takes 1 hour to build.
  • The timer runs on the city's servers, so it keeps counting even after you close the game — come back and your floor is ready.
  • A house can grow up to 5 floors.

Build a second house

Dedicated builders can claim a second home. It unlocks once your first house is fully grown, rewarding you for seeing a project through.

  • Finish all 5 floors of your first house first.
  • Then you may build one more — two houses per player, maximum.

Open a business

Next to your home you can open a small business — a bakery, café, workshop and more, based on your house's style. Stock it with goods and other players' residents will come and shop.

  • New shops appear instantly for every player, live — no page refresh needed.
  • Set your prices and keep your shelves stocked to keep customers coming.
  • A small marketplace fee applies to each sale between players.

Family & daily life

Your house is home to a little family that lives on even while you are away. Keep them supplied and happy — food, rest, and comfort all matter. A well-kept home shows in the city.

  • Residents have needs that change over time.
  • Fund your household budget and buy supplies to keep everyone thriving.
  • More floors mean a bigger family and more life around your home.

Decorate & collect

Step inside and make each floor yours with furniture and playful crypto-culture items. Fill your yard, collect rare pieces, and give your home a personality that stands out on the street.

One living city

Everything happens on one shared map in real time. New houses, new shops, and new floors appear for everyone as they happen. Explore your neighbors, follow the roads, and watch a small town slowly become a city — built by the whole community, together.

My Hood is for building, collecting, and belonging — not for financial return. Have fun and make your mark.
